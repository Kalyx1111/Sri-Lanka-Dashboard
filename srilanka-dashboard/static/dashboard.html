Map basis: Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection centred on the island, drawn from Natural Earth boundaries, province lines, rivers and sea labels. The frame is Sri Lanka plus 150 km on every side (about 78.3 to 83.3 degrees east and 4.6 to 11.2 degrees north). Sri Lanka is shaded. Neighbouring land at the edge of the frame appears as a plain outline only; nothing about any neighbouring state is plotted, and no foreign deployment is described beyond the Chinese-linked facilities and visits listed. Nuclear power plants, nuclear storage, missile bases and space-launch sites are absent because none exists in Sri Lanka in the sources reviewed; the candidate sites for a first nuclear plant are not published. Most positions are approximate; each tooltip gives its source and whether it is verified in this build. Reported events and claims are attributed. Events reflect reports up to 10 October 2026.

Sources: Ransomware.live (leak-site claims - group claims, not verified facts); Telegram threat-intel channels; and dark-web RSS sources reached through Tor that you verify yourself (data/onion_sources.json).
To read onion sources you need Tor on this PC: open Tor Browser (it listens on port 9150) or install and start the Tor service (port 9050). Keep it running, then press Refresh. Nothing is needed for the clear-web and Telegram sources. This dashboard never opens .onion pages in a browser and does not search or crawl the dark web.
