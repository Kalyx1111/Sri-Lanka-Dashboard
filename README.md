# Sri Lanka Dashboard

**Locally Hosted OSINT & Geospatial Intelligence Platform**

Sri Lanka Dashboard is a locally hosted OSINT platform for mapping strategic infrastructure, military establishments, maritime routes, energy assets, undersea cables and regional developments relating to Sri Lanka.

***

#### Key Features

- **70 strategic points** with status, date and source information.
- **15 intelligence categories** covering military, maritime, cyber, economic and regional developments.
- **Interactive map** covering Sri Lanka and a surrounding 150 km geographic frame.
- **Military infrastructure** including selected naval, air force and army establishments.
- **Strategic infrastructure** covering ports, fuel facilities, refineries and undersea cables.
- **Cyber and maritime intelligence** with selected institutions, shipping routes and chokepoints.
- **Local operation** through a Windows batch launcher.

***

#### Geographic Coverage

- Sri Lanka and approximately 150 km of surrounding area.
- Equal-area map projection.
- Map canvas: 1,300 × 1,731 pixels.
- Neighbouring land at the map boundary appears only as a plain outline, without plotted towns, ports, airports or rivers.

#### Strategic Infrastructure

- **Naval:** Colombo, Trincomalee, Galle, Karainagar and selected smaller establishments.
- **Air Force:** Katunayake, China Bay, Ratmalana, Hingurakgoda, Palaly, Vavuniya and Mattala.
- **Army:** Akuregoda, Panagoda, selected northern and eastern headquarters, and Elephant Pass.
- **Ports & Maritime:** Colombo, Hambantota, Colombo Port City, Palk Strait and southern sea lanes.
- **Energy:** Trincomalee tank farm, Sapugaskanda refinery, Lakvijaya power station and selected proposed projects.
- **Undersea Cables & Cyber:** Selected cable landing stations and Sri Lanka CERT references.

***

#### Installation & Launch

1. Extract `srilanka-dashboard-v1.zip`.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Run `start.bat`.
4. Open the dashboard in your browser.

**Local URL:** `http://127.0.0.1:8835`

***

#### Testing & Limitations

- **37 Python tests passed.**
- **3 browser test suites passed.**
- The packaged ZIP was rebuilt and tested in a clean folder.
- 13 points are flagged as unverified in this build.
- Some locations are approximate.
- Live news feeds were not tested because the build environment had no internet access.
- Proposed projects and historical events should not be interpreted as confirmation of current operational status.
